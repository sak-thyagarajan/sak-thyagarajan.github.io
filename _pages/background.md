Before joining the University of Sydney, I was a postdoctoral fellow at NTT Research and previously worked with [Elaine Shi](http://elaineshi.com/) at Carnegie Mellon University. I completed my PhD at the Chair of Applied Cryptography at [FAU Erlangen–Nürnberg](https://www.fau.eu), Germany, under the supervision of [Dominique Schröder](https://dominique-schroeder.de/).

I received my MSc in Computer Science from [Saarland University](https://www.uni-saarland.de/en/home.html), Germany, and my B.Tech. from the [National Institute of Technology, Trichy](https://www.nitt.edu), India.

I also remain deeply grateful to the schools that shaped my early years: [DAV Boys Senior Secondary School, Gopalapuram, Chennai](https://bgpm.davchennai.org), where I studied from 2008 to 2011, and [DAV BHEL School, Ranipet](https://bhelranipet.davchennai.org), where I spent my formative school years from 1997 to 2008.
