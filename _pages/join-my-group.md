<div class="join-page">
  <p>
    Welcome! If you like security, cryptography, and blockchains, and you enjoy turning
    “this should work” into “this provably works”, you might fit right in.
  </p>

  <section class="join-section">
    <h2>What we work on</h2>
    <p>
      Broadly, we work on cryptography and secure protocols, especially where
      rigorous theory meets real systems.
    </p>

    <div class="join-grid">
      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>MPC & threshold cryptography</h3>
        <p>
          Secure computation, distributed protocols, threshold signatures, and protocols
          with strong adversarial guarantees.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>Blockchain security and fairness</h3>
        <p>
          Cryptographic tools for blockchains, cryptocurrencies, fair exchange, adaptor
          signatures, and protocol-level privacy.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>Post-quantum cryptography</h3>
        <p>
          Quantum-resistant protocols, signatures, secure computation, and constructions
          that remain efficient enough to be useful.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

    <p>
      If you enjoy proofs, protocols, and occasionally proving that a “clever hack” is
      actually a bug, we will probably get along.
    </p>

  </section>

  <section class="join-section">
    <h2>Who should reach out?</h2>

    <p>I am happy to hear from:</p>

    <ul class="join-list">
      <li>Prospective PhD students</li>
      <li>Honours and MSc thesis students</li>
      <li>Strong coursework students interested in research projects or reading groups</li>
      <li>Students with interests in cryptography, security, blockchains, or privacy</li>
    </ul>

    <div class="join-note">
      <strong>Good signs, not strict requirements:</strong>
      comfort with mathematical thinking, some programming experience, curiosity,
      persistence, and a willingness to read carefully.
    </div>

  </section>

  <section class="join-section">
    <h2>What you might do here</h2>

    <div class="join-grid two">
      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>Read deeply</h3>
        <p>
          Read and discuss papers, learn to extract the core idea, and translate
          crypto-speak into clear English.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>Build rigorously</h3>
        <p>
          Design protocols, define threat models, prove security, and understand where
          assumptions enter the picture.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>Prototype when useful</h3>
        <p>
          Build small prototypes or run benchmarks when implementation helps clarify
          feasibility.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="join-card">
        <h3>Write clearly</h3>
        <p>
          Learn how to turn ideas into papers, talks, and submissions to strong venues.
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>

  </section>

  <section class="join-section">
    <h2>How to reach out</h2>

    <p>
    Email is best. Before emailing, you may want to read one paper from the area that interests you and tell me what you found exciting, confusing, or questionable. A thoughtful paragraph is more useful than a generic statement of interest. </p>

    <p>
       To make sure your message does not get lost, please use a clear
      subject line:
    </p>

    <div class="join-email-box">
      <div class="join-label">Subject line</div>
      <code>Prospective &lt;PhD/Honours/MSc&gt; student – &lt;Your Name&gt; – &lt;Topic&gt;</code>

      <div class="join-label">Example</div>
      <code>Prospective PhD student – Satoshi Nakamoto – MPC/Threshold Crypto</code>
    </div>

    <p>Please include:</p>

    <ol class="join-list">
      <li>Your CV as a PDF</li>
      <li>Your academic transcript; unofficial is fine to start</li>
      <li>
        A short note of 5–10 sentences explaining what topics excite you, why this
        group or direction interests you, and any relevant experience.
      </li>
    </ol>

    <p>
      Optional but helpful: links to GitHub, writing samples, a short research statement,
      or papers/projects you are proud of.
    </p>

    <div class="join-note">
      I will always prefer a clear message over a long one. Concise is the mantra.
    </div>

  </section>

  <section class="join-section">
    <h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

    <div class="join-card" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">Do I need to know cryptography already?</h3>
      <p>
        No, but you should be comfortable with mathematical reasoning and willing to read proofs carefully.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="join-card" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">Do I need to be a strong programmer?</h3>
      <p>
        It is helpful, but not required. Some projects are proof-heavy and purely theoretical, while others involve building prototypes and running benchmarks. But this skill can be picked up along the way (Hint: There are AI tools to help you here.)
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="join-card" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">Can undergraduate students work with you?</h3>
      <p>
        Yes, through honours programs, coursework research projects, reading groups, or summer-style projects, depending on my current availability.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="join-card" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">What should I read before emailing?</h3>
      <p>
        A great starting point is to look at recent papers spanning MPC/threshold cryptography, blockchain fairness, and post-quantum cryptography. Mentioning a specific paper from my recent publications that caught your eye is always a great way to start a conversation.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="join-card" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">Are there funded PhD positions?</h3>
      <p>
        Funding generally depends on university scholarship rounds (such as the RTP at the University of Sydney) and specific project grants. Strong applicants should contact me early so we can discuss timelines, project fit, and scholarship applications.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="join-card" style="margin-bottom: 1rem;">
      <h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.5rem; font-size: 1.05rem;">What makes a good first email?</h3>
      <p>
        Following the subject line format and providing the materials requested above! Please do not send a generic email; I care much more about a specific, thoughtful note regarding why our interests align than a long, pasted template.
      </p>
    </div>

  </section>
